GATE 10

Journal
GATE 10 Monte Carlo Particle Transport Simulation: I. Development and New Features
Sarrut D., Arbor N., Baudier T., Bert J., Chatzipapas K., Favaretto M., … (33 authors) · Physics in Medicine & Biology, 71(1), 015042, 2026
DOIHAL (open access)PubMed
Journal
GATE 10 Monte Carlo Particle Transport Simulation: II. Architecture and Innovations
Krah N., Arbor N., Baudier T., Bert J., Chatzipapas K., Favaretto M., …, Sarrut D. (33 authors) · Physics in Medicine & Biology, 71(1), 015043, 2026
DOIHAL (open access)PubMed

GATE 10 is a complete re-implementation of GATE, the open-source Monte Carlo platform for medical physics built on Geant4. Simulations are now plain Python scripts, run multithreaded, and GATE can be embedded as a library in other software. Part I presents the new features, Part II the architecture behind them.

FFAA

Journal
Free flight angular acceptance (FFAA) variance reduction technique for SPECT Monte Carlo simulations
Sarrut D., Etxebeste A., Jacquet M., Létang J. M. · Physics in Medicine and Biology, 71(9), 095009, 2026
DOIHAL (open access)PubMed

A variance reduction technique that makes SPECT Monte Carlo simulations about 100 to 200 times faster, without pre-computed detector response or training: primary photons are forced towards the detector, scattered photons are split and kept only when they can reach it, and the collimator and detector stay fully simulated.

Jacobian-Free Unrolling

Journal
Jacobian-Free Unrolling: memory efficient unrolled network for 3D SPECT Reconstruction
Constanza C., Kaprelian T., Etxebeste A., Sarrut D. · IEEE Transactions on Radiation and Plasma Medical Sciences, 2026
DOIHAL (open access)

Deep "unrolled" reconstruction networks are hard to train for fully 3D SPECT because they need too much GPU memory. Jacobian-Free Unrolling back-propagates through the last iteration only, which makes 3D training possible, with better images than OSEM and MAPEM on simulated, phantom and patient ¹⁷⁷Lu data.

Partial volume correction with deep learning

Journal
Partial Volume Correction of SPECT Images With Deep Learning Trained on Synthetic Data
Kaprélian T., Etxebeste A., Sarrut D. · IEEE Transactions on Radiation and Plasma Medical Sciences, 1–1, 2025
DOIHAL (open access)

Partial volume effects blur small structures in SPECT and bias activity estimates, a key issue for ¹⁷⁷Lu dosimetry. Neural networks trained only on synthetic data correct them in the projections, in the image, or in both (hybrid); the hybrid version gave the best noise/resolution trade-off, with errors reduced by a third on a simulated patient.

Predicting PSA50 response

Journal
Predicting PSA50 Response to 177Lu-PSMA Therapy Using Machine Learning and Automated Total Tumor Volume
Rios-Sanchez E., Giraudet A.-L., Sanchez-Lajusticia A., Pretet V., Paquet E., Baudier T., Badel J.-N., Sarrut D. · EJNMMI Physics, 12(1), 95, 2025
DOIHAL (open access)PubMed

Can a PET/CT made before treatment predict which patients will respond to ¹⁷⁷Lu-PSMA therapy? The total tumour volume is extracted automatically from ⁶⁸Ga-PSMA PET/CT, and machine-learning models predict the PSA50 response of 139 patients as well as existing nomograms.

Reducing the imaging workload of ¹⁷⁷Lu dosimetry

Journal
A review of 177Lu dosimetry workflows: how to reduce the imaging workloads?
Vergnaud L., Dewaraja Y. K., Giraudet A.-L., Badel J.-N., Sarrut D. · EJNMMI Physics, 11(1), 65, 2024
DOIHAL (open access)PubMed

Dosimetry can personalise ¹⁷⁷Lu therapy, but it needs several SPECT/CT scans after injection. This review describes how to reduce that burden, with fewer or shorter acquisitions down to a single time point, with their assumptions and limits, for ¹⁷⁷Lu-DOTATATE and ¹⁷⁷Lu-PSMA.

Photon source model for alpha emitters

Journal
A Photon Source Model for Alpha-Emitter Radionuclides
Sarrut D., Etxebeste A., Létang J. M. · Physics in Medicine & Biology, 69(9), 095009, 2024
DOIHAL (open access)PubMed

PHID emits directly the photons of the whole decay chain of alpha-emitter radionuclides such as ²²⁵Ac, with the right energies and timing (Bateman equations), instead of simulating every decay. About 30 times faster in a typical example, for any alpha emitter in Geant4, and open source in GATE 10.

GAN for SPECT Monte Carlo simulation

Journal
Modeling Complex Particles Phase Space with GAN for Monte Carlo SPECT Simulations: A Proof of Concept
Sarrut D., Etxebeste A., Krah N., Létang J.-M. · Physics in Medicine and Biology, 2021
DOIHAL (open access)PubMed

A generative adversarial network (GAN) learns the particles exiting the patient. Combined with a neural network for the detector response, it simulates a full rotating SPECT acquisition without tracking the particles in the patient, with reduced computation time (proof of concept).

GAN for compact beam source models

Journal
Generative Adversarial Networks (GAN) for Compact Beam Source Modelling in Monte Carlo Simulations
Sarrut D., Krah N., Letang J.-M. · Physics in Medicine and Biology, 64(21), 2019
DOIHAL (open access)PubMed

A phase-space file of several gigabytes is replaced by a compact GAN of about 10 MB that generates particles on demand. Dose distributions for linac beams and brachytherapy seeds agree with the reference within 1 % voxel by voxel.

Voxel-based multi-model fitting (VoMM)

Journal
Voxel-based multi-model fitting method for modelling time activity curves in SPECT images
Sarrut D., Halty A., Badel J., Ferrer L., Bardiès M. · Medical Physics, 44, 6280-6288, 2017
DOIHAL (open access)PubMed

Voxel-based dosimetry needs a fit of the time-activity curve of every voxel. Instead of the same model for all voxels, VoMM selects for each voxel the most appropriate model among several (Akaike criterion): errors on the time-integrated activity below 5 % instead of over 10 % in simulations, and 90 % of the voxels fitted on patient data.

Lymph node stations from relative positions

Journal
Learning directional relative positions between mediastinal lymph node stations and organs
Sarrut D., Rit S., Claude L., Pinho R., Pitson G., Bouilhol G., Lynch R. · Medical Physics, 41(6), 061905, 2014
DOIHAL (open access)PubMed

Mediastinal lymph node stations are defined by their position relative to the organs around them. These directional relations are learned automatically from expert delineations, then used to segment the stations on new CT images (mean Dice 66 %). The database of the five reference cases is publicly available.

Spatio-temporal motion estimation in 4D CT

Journal
Spatio-Temporal Motion Estimation for Respiratory-Correlated Imaging of the Lungs
Vandemeulebroucke J., Rit S., Kybic J., Clarysse P., Sarrut D. · Medical Physics, 38(1), 2011
DOIHAL (open access)PubMed

Estimating lung motion in 4D CT is difficult because of artefacts. Instead of registering each breathing phase separately, the motion of every point over the whole respiratory cycle is modelled as one smooth cyclic trajectory (B-splines in space and time), which is more robust to artefacts.
